# TriLink Business Architecture

TriLink operates as one unified business platform with specialist divisions.

## Divisions

- TriLink Trade
- TriLink Global
- TriLink Digital
- TriLink Logistics
- TriLink Partners

The divisions are intentionally kept under the TriLink master brand.

This allows the company to expand into additional services without creating
separate disconnected brands or websites.

Business claims should remain factual and should not imply licences,
certifications, partnerships or operational capabilities that have not been
verified.
