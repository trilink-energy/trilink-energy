# 22.34 INCIDENT RESPONSE
# ============================================================

If a serious problem occurs:

1. STOP affected automation.
2. Protect customer/business data.
3. Protect financial systems.
4. Identify the affected workflow.
5. Review audit logs.
6. Determine the cause.
7. Correct the problem.
8. Test the correction.
9. Resume only when safe.
10. Record the incident.

CEO authority remains available at every stage.
